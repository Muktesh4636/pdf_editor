PDF Edit FAQ

Go to PDF Forge (pdfforge.store), upload your PDF by drag-and-drop or by clicking the button, then click any text to edit it. Click Download in the toolbar when done — no need to press Enter.
We store a copy of the original PDF when you open it and a copy of the exported PDF after you save edits. Editing itself happens in your browser.
Completely free — upload, edit, and download PDFs with no cost and no registration.
PDF Editor works on all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge, on both desktop and mobile devices.
This editor only changes real PDF text (fonts that the file stores as text). Most handwriting and scanned writing is stored as a picture, not as letters — the browser cannot "analyse" and recreate your personal writing. For that you would need dedicated handwriting software or machine-learning tools, not a simple website. If your PDF is a photo of handwriting, re-type with a standard or cursive font for an approximate look, or use separate OCR software.
